About

Brian Busch, GISP

I am a GIS professional focused on cartography, spatial analysis, and practical map products. This site collects selected maps, project work, and short notes about how the work was made.

The goal is simple: keep finished maps, project context, data notes, and design decisions in one place that is easy to update and easy to share.

What I publish here

  • Cartography and print-map layouts
  • GIS project summaries and web-map concepts
  • Spatial analysis notes and data-method writeups
  • Small cartography notes about labels, palettes, hierarchy, and simplification
