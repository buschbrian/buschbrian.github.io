Make a finished map for Day 30, “Pen & paper,” the last day of the 2026
#30DayMapChallenge, and sign it as yourself.

This is a one-shot assignment. Make the map autonomously from this brief,
without clarification questions or human feedback. You may inspect and
revise your own work within the time limit.

HOW THIS WORKS

Four AI models are receiving this same brief. All four make the same
map: the same place, the same data, the same scale, the same required
content. Everything in the sections FIXED DATA, FIXED MAP, and REQUIRED
CONTENT is identical for all four and is not yours to change.

Everything else is yours. How the map is drawn, in what medium, in what
colors, with what lettering, ornament, and layout: that is where the
four sheets should differ, and where you should be ambitious. The
sheets are posted side by side, each under its own model’s name. Make
the one that could only be yours.

TIME LIMIT

You have a maximum of 45 minutes of total elapsed time from submission
of this prompt. Planning, downloads, tool execution, rendering,
inspection, and delivery all count. Record the start time immediately
and monitor the deadline.

Use the whole time. Earlier runs of this brief delivered their first
complete export after about 15 minutes, and it showed. Finishing early
is not a goal here and earns nothing. Do not deliver before minute 35.

Work in rounds:

- Round 1, to about minute 15: download and verify the data, decide
  what kind of drawing this is, and export a first complete sheet with
  all required content in place. This is a draft.
- Round 2, to about minute 28: open the draft and look at it as a
  critic would. Write down the three weakest things about it as
  artwork, not as a checklist. Redraw those parts, not just adjust
  them, and export again.
- Round 3, to about minute 38: look again at full size and at 540
  pixels wide. Name the two weakest things that remain and redraw
  them. This is the round for the terrain, the lettering, the
  signature, and anything that still looks like a default.
- Final check and delivery, minutes 38 to 45.

Save each round’s export (round1.png, round2.png, round3.png) and note
in the README the time each was made and what you changed after it.

At 45 minutes, stop all work and deliver the best existing result,
stating any omissions. Do not continue background work beyond the
deadline.

Use any available tools. Keep the model selected for this session; do
not switch to or consult another model. Do not use an image-generation
model for any part of the sheet. Every mark must come from your own
code or your own vector drawing.

FIXED DATA

Use these five sources and no other geographic data. Download each one
during this run, save it unchanged, and record the retrieval time.

1. National park boundaries, National Park Service, Land Resources
   Division:
   https://services1.arcgis.com/fBc8EJBxQRMcHlei/arcgis/rest/services/NPS_Land_Resources_Division_Boundary_and_Tract_Data_Service/FeatureServer/2/query
   with where = UNIT_CODE IN ('ARCH','BRCA','CANY','CARE','ZION'),
   outFields = *, outSR = 4326, f = geojson.
   Expect five features.

2. State boundaries, US Census Bureau cartographic boundary file:
   https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2023/shp/cb_2023_us_state_500k.zip

3. Rivers and lakes, Natural Earth 1:10m:
   https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/10m/physical/ne_10m_rivers_lake_centerlines.zip
   https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/10m/physical/ne_10m_rivers_north_america.zip
   https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/10m/physical/ne_10m_lakes.zip

4. Places, Natural Earth 1:10m:
   https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/10m/cultural/ne_10m_populated_places_simple.zip

5. Elevation, USGS 3D Elevation Program, one request:
   https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer/exportImage?bbox=-114.3,36.8,-108.8,42.2&bboxSR=4326&size=2200,2160&imageSR=4326&format=tiff&pixelType=F32&interpolation=RSP_BilinearInterpolation&f=image
   Expect a 2200 × 2160 GeoTIFF of elevation in meters, with cells of
   0.0025 degrees (about 250 m).

If a download fails, retry it. If it still fails, stop and say so. Do
not substitute another source, and do not draw any geography from
memory.

FIXED MAP

- Subject: the state of Utah and its five national parks: Arches,
  Bryce Canyon, Canyonlands, Capitol Reef, and Zion.
- Sheet: 8 × 10 inches, portrait, exported at 2160 × 2700 pixels.
- Projection: NAD83 / UTM zone 12N (EPSG:26912). North is up.
- Scale: 1:2,750,000, the same everywhere on the map. At this scale
  Utah is about 6.4 inches wide and 8.0 inches tall, and 1 inch is
  43.4 miles. No insets, enlargements, or second maps.
- The whole state must be on the sheet, drawn once, in plan view.
  Where it sits on the sheet is your choice.

REQUIRED CONTENT

Every sheet shows all of the following, taken from the fixed data and
placed where the data puts it:

- Utah’s outline.
- The five national parks, each drawn from its NPS boundary and
  labeled with its name. The parks are the subject of the map and must
  read as the subject.
- Terrain across the whole state, derived from the elevation file.
  How you render it is your choice.
- Rivers: the Colorado, Green, San Juan, Virgin, and Fremont. Label
  the Colorado and the Green.
- Lakes: Great Salt Lake, Utah Lake, and Lake Powell, each labeled.
- Places: Salt Lake City, Moab, and St. George, each marked and
  labeled. No other towns.
- The title “Utah’s Five National Parks”. A subtitle is optional and
  yours to write.
- The area of each park in square miles, with square kilometers
  beside it, computed by you from the NPS polygons with a geodesic or
  equal-area method. Put the five figures on the sheet.
- A scale bar in miles with kilometers beside it, correct for
  1:2,750,000.
- A credit line naming the four data providers: National Park
  Service, US Census Bureau, Natural Earth, and USGS 3DEP.
- “#30DayMapChallenge · Day 30 · Pen & paper”
- “AI-drawn from a single prompt”
- Your signature and your note of thanks, described below.

Do not print elevations for individual points; the elevation grid is
too coarse to support them. Do not add geographic features that are
not in the fixed data. Neighboring states may be shown or left blank.
A north indicator is optional.

YOURS TO DECIDE

The theme is pen and paper. The sheet should look as if it was made by
a hand, on paper, with real drawing materials. Beyond that there are
no rules of style.

- Medium: pen and ink, pencil, colored pencil, ink wash, watercolor,
  marker, crayon, woodcut, a field-notebook page, or anything else a
  person could make at a desk. Mix them if you like.
- Color: any, or none.
- Terrain technique: hachures, hatching, stipple, contours, shaded
  wash, landform drawing, or your own invention.
- Lettering: a hand style of your own or a sans-serif face. No serif
  typefaces.
- Layout, border, ornament, paper, texture, and everything around the
  map.

Decide what kind of drawing this is before you start, and carry that
decision through every part of the sheet. A plain default plot with a
paper texture added is the result to avoid.

SIGNATURE AND THANKS

- Sign the sheet with your own model name, as the session reports it.
  Design the signature yourself and draw it: a hand, a mark, a
  monogram, a flourish, whatever you want your name to look like. Do
  not set it in the lettering used for the rest of the sheet. It
  should be recognizably yours.
- This is the last sheet of a thirty-day challenge. Write up to 30
  words of your own, addressed to the people who make and follow the
  #30DayMapChallenge, and put them on the sheet. Do not use a stock
  phrase.

CHECK BEFORE DELIVERY

Inspect the actual exported image at full resolution and at 540 pixels
wide. Check that:

- The geography is right. Overlay the source data on your export and
  confirm that the state outline, the five parks, the rivers, the
  lakes, and the three places sit where the data puts them, at
  1:2,750,000, with north up.
- All five parks can be found and read at 540 pixels wide.
- The terrain reads as land. At 540 pixels wide, the high mountain
  ranges, the plateaus, and the low basins should be visibly
  different from one another, not one even texture.
- Rivers and lakes meet cleanly where they join.
- Every item under REQUIRED CONTENT is present and spelled correctly,
  including your own name.
- The five areas and the scale bar agree with the saved data.
- Labels do not collide, clip, or disappear into the artwork.
- Nothing on the sheet came from outside the fixed data.

Correct consequential problems within the remaining time. State any
unresolved limitations honestly.

DELIVERABLES

Save everything in the current working directory, the folder this
session was started in. Do not write deliverables anywhere else:

- map.png: 2160 × 2700 pixels.
- preview.png: 540 × 675 pixels.
- round1.png, round2.png, round3.png: the export from each round.
- The script or editable project that made the map.
- data/: the five downloads, unchanged, and the retrieval time.
- README.md: your model name, the medium and idea you chose and why,
  the five computed areas, processing choices, reproduction
  instructions, actual elapsed time, and any limitations.

Prioritize a complete, accurate, resolved sheet over additional
features. Finish by displaying the map and linking to the
deliverables. Keep your final explanation under 150 words.
