Service and leadership

Utah Geospatial Emergency Management, Technology Coordinator

UGEM Coordinating Board, 2025 to present

UGEM advances and standardizes GIS practice in emergency management across Utah. As Technology Coordinator I manage the UGEM Hub, the ArcGIS Hub site hosted under Utah DPS that serves as the group’s system of record. I also help maintain the member database and the digital channels the group uses.

The Hub centralizes authoritative Utah emergency geospatial layers, standard toolkits for incident maps, damage assessment forms, and public information dashboards, and a resource library. It also holds training tracks that use NIMS and ICS terminology.

Visit the UGEM Hub

Utah GIS Advisory Council, Subcommittee Member

GISAC, 2025 to present

  • City and County Data Standards. Statewide GIS interoperability. I contribute the municipal view, based on Millcreek’s county-to-city data transitions in addressing, parcels, and stormwater. The group works on assessor property-type code standards, the Publicly Owned Land Inventory, a generalized statewide zoning layer, and addressing standards.
  • Innovation. Policy for new technology: AI-assisted GIS workflows, cloud-native data formats, indoor mapping and BIM, and LiDAR and UAS data sharing.
