30 Day Map Challenge 2024 — AI

AI-Generated Fictional Map

A ChatGPT-generated "map" of fictional Utah water resources, included for what it gets wrong — not for what it gets right.

Challenge30 Day Map Challenge 2024
GeneratorChatGPT / OpenAI
GIS software usedNone
AccuracyIntentionally unreliable

The theme is simply "AI," and the most honest way to engage with it was to hand the whole map over to ChatGPT and see what came back: a set of fictional visualizations of Utah's lakes, rivers, and reservoirs under hypothetical water-resource change. No ArcGIS, no real data — just a prompt.

An experiment, not a map

Look closely and the cracks show immediately: place names come out garbled ("Great Saat Salt Lake"), invented labels sit next to real ones, and none of it is backed by an actual dataset. It's visually confident and geographically meaningless at the same time — which is exactly the point.

Why it's worth keeping on the site

AI image generation can produce something that looks like a map in seconds, but it's a reminder of what that shortcut skips: real data provenance, coordinate accuracy, and the judgment a trained GIS professional brings to turning raw data into something people can actually rely on. Interesting to look at; not something to plan around.

Created for the “AI” theme of the 2024 #30DayMapChallenge using ChatGPT/OpenAI image generation — no geographic data was used or represented accurately.
