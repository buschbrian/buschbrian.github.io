30 Day Map Challenge 2024 — Polygons

Utah Federal Land Ownership

The patchwork of federally managed land across Utah — BLM, National Parks, Wilderness Areas — rendered in Esri's "Firefly" style.

Challenge30 Day Map Challenge 2024
Data sourceUGRC & SITLA
StyleFirefly, via John Nelson
ToolsArcGIS Pro

Polygons are where land management gets political, and few datasets make that clearer than land ownership. Utah's federal land — BLM territory, National Parks, Wilderness Areas, and more — forms an intricate patchwork that shapes the state's geography, economy, and recreation every bit as much as its physical terrain does.

A style built to make polygons glow

The map uses John Nelson's "Firefly" style, built for exactly this kind of dense, overlapping polygon data — dark background, luminous edges — so ownership categories stay legible even where boundaries get complicated.

Matching a reference map

Rather than invent a new color scheme from scratch, the palette was matched to the Utah School and Institutional Trust Lands Administration (SITLA)'s own land ownership map, so this version reads as a companion to an already-authoritative reference rather than a competing one.

Created for the “Polygons” theme of the 2024 #30DayMapChallenge, using UGRC and SITLA land ownership data.
