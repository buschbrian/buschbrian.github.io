30 Day Map Challenge 2024 — Vintage Style

Vintage Atlas Style

Utah's urban areas and rivers, styled after an 1854 atlas — proof that a 170-year-old aesthetic still holds up against a modern basemap.

Challenge30 Day Map Challenge 2024
Style referenceEsri "Physical Atlas," via John Nelson
Inspiration1854 David Rumsey Map Collection
ToolsArcGIS Pro

The vintage-style day is really an exercise in restraint: modern GIS software makes it easy to render crisp, high-contrast maps, and the challenge is deliberately going the other direction. This map takes Utah's urban areas and rivers and runs them through Esri's "Physical Atlas" style — John Nelson's take on the hand-drawn, engraved look of the 1854 David Rumsey Map Collection.

Why the old look still works

Vintage atlas styles trade precision for texture — muted tones, engraved-line water features, hand-lettered-feeling type — and it turns out that texture still reads clearly on a screen, not just on paper. It's a useful reminder that clarity doesn't require a sterile default basemap.

Created for the “Vintage Style” theme of the 2024 #30DayMapChallenge, using Esri's Physical Atlas style.
