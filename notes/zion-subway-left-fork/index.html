The walk

On November 12, 2026, I walked the Left Fork of North Creek to the Subway. The Subway is a curved, tunnel-like part of the canyon. The National Park Service requires a wilderness permit for it.

The walk is about 7 miles out and back, as OpenStreetMap maps the route. My GPS track started 0.9 miles down the trail, so the mapped route fills that gap.
