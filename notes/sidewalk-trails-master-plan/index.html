Sidewalk & Trails Master Plan, Millcreek | Esri UC Map Gallery Finalist 2026

Turning sidewalk gaps into a connected trail network

A citywide map of designated centers, paths, and trails for Millcreek's Sidewalk & Trails Master Plan, recognized as a finalist in Esri's 2026 Virtual Map Gallery.

AwardFinalist, Envisioning the Future
AuthorBrian Busch
OrganizationCity of Millcreek
Gallery2026 Esri User Conference

The Millcreek Sidewalk & Trails Master Plan — Designated Centers, Paths & Trails presents a high-level snapshot of the city's vision for a more connected, walkable, and bike-friendly community. The map illustrates how shared-use paths, enhanced urban paths, existing and proposed trails, waterways, trailheads, parks, pedestrian districts, and village centers come together to form a connected mobility network across Millcreek.

Designated centers and gateways

The design highlights three key gateway and activity areas: City Center, Meadowbrook, and the Southern Gateway. Each area is supported by corridor improvements that strengthen pedestrian access, neighborhood connectivity, and local identity.

Paths and trail typology

By pairing a detailed citywide trail map with center-specific renderings and path typology diagrams, this submission communicates both the broader network vision and the human-scale improvements that will shape Millcreek's streets and centers — shared-use paths, enhanced urban paths, waterway trails, and general trails, each responding to a different context.

Looking ahead

Beyond the current network, the plan flags creek and canal trail opportunities and future east bench trail connections, including completion of the Bonneville Shoreline Trail through Millcreek.

Recognition

Esri listed the submission as a finalist in the Envisioning the Future award category for the 2026 User Conference Virtual Map Gallery. The map was built with Aubrey Larsen (Landmark Design) and Fred Lutze, PE, as part of Millcreek's effort to better understand sidewalk gaps, trail priorities, and future connections throughout the city.

Source: Esri User Conference Virtual Map Gallery submission.
