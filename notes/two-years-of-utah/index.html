2 Years of Utah in 136 Photographs

A Reel of the Dust & Starlight places map. The photographs land on a map of Utah in the order of their capture dates.

Challenge dateNovember 16, 2026

Photographs in the order of capture

Dust & Starlight shows its photographs on a map. This Reel plays that map back as a story. The photographs land on Utah one at a time, from June 2024 to November 2026.

The Reel shows 24 photographs in full, for two seconds each. A small map collects the points of all 136 photographs. When a photograph arrives, the points made since the last one arrive with it. The date and the count move with them.

The Reel ends on the full map, then a credits card.

Watch the Reel

What the numbers mean

  • 136 edited photographs sit on the site's map of Utah.
  • 123 are at places the site records as Utah. 13 are just over the line: 7 in Arizona at Coyote Buttes South and 6 in Nevada at Great Basin National Park and its overlooks.
  • 66 photographs have a published spot. 70 have only the center of their place.
  • 23 photographs share one point, at The Wave and Wire Pass.

A map point shows where a photograph was made, to the site's published precision. The site rounds a spot to about 1 km. It rounds a place center to about 11 km. A point means "around here". It does not mean where anyone stood.

Who made it

Sam plans the trips and finds the places. Brian makes and edits the photographs, and built the map motion. The 24 photographs in the Reel came from the 136, picked for a spread of trips and subjects.

Method

  • The photograph and place records come from the Dust & Starlight site, read from its repository on November 10, 2026. The 205 photographs on the site's map match the "205 photos on the map" total on its places page for that date. The Reel uses the 136 on the Utah map.
  • The map is drawn for this Reel. It shows the five national parks in Utah from National Park Service boundaries, and shaded relief from the USGS 3D Elevation Program.
  • Each point uses the published spot of a photograph. A photograph without a spot uses the center of its place.
  • The Utah outline comes from Utah's straight latitude and longitude borders. Short lines show where the borders of the states next to Utah continue.
  • GDAL changes the elevation data to the projection of the map. The size of a point shows how many photographs share it.
  • Photographs sort by capture date, then by capture time. 83 photographs have no time, so the order within one day is not exact.
  • A Python script draws each frame with Pillow and NumPy. FFmpeg joins the frames into video.

Inside the script

A Python script, day16_landings.py, places each photograph on the map. The site draws its maps in Web Mercator, so the script uses the same formula. It turns a longitude and latitude into a pixel on the map picture.

Data notes

  • This Reel shows only photographs that Brian edited from his Sony camera and his drone, and published on the site. It does not show unedited photographs or photographs from phones.
  • The site publishes 231 photographs. 205 have a place on its map. This Reel uses the 136 on the Utah map. The other 69 are in Idaho, Montana, Wyoming, California and Washington.
  • The 13 photographs over the Utah line sit just outside the outline.
  • The counts are for November 10, 2026. The site adds photographs often.

Data and credit

The photographs are from Dust & Starlight. The National Park Service provides the park boundaries. The USGS 3D Elevation Program provides the terrain.
