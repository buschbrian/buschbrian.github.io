About

Maps and computer science by day. Photography and more maps on the weekends.

During the week I’m a Senior GIS Analyst for Millcreek. I write the code behind the city’s planning maps, train city staff in GIS, and keep the data under those maps correct. Earlier I set up the city’s GNSS field program and drone program, built its stormwater inventory, and helped move staff off ArcMap.

On weekends I’m usually on a dirt road in southern Utah, out under dark skies with a camera, or on a winter trail run. I’ll take the long way if it has a better view. The photos end up at Dust and Starlight, and the places tend to end up on a map.
